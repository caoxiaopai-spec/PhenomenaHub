## 📚 About

PhenomenaHub is an open-source knowledge repository dedicated to collecting, organizing, and documenting **laws** and **effects** from all fields of human knowledge. From psychology to physics, from economics to computer science, from philosophy to marketing – this repository aims to be a comprehensive reference for students, researchers, professionals, and curious minds.

**Repository:** https://github.com/caoxiaopai-spec/PhenomenaHub

## 🎯 Mission

Our mission is to:
- **Comprehensively collect** all known laws and effects across disciplines
- **Systematically organize** knowledge by fields and categories
- **Provide detailed explanations** with origins, applications, and cross-references
- **Maintain bilingual content** in English and Chinese for global accessibility
- **Foster community contributions** to keep the repository growing and updated

## 🗂️ Core Domains

PhenomenaHub currently provides extensive coverage in the following ten foundational domains:

- [**Psychology**](docs/Psychology.md) — Cognitive processes, behavioral patterns, mental models
- [**Economics**](docs/Economics.md) — Market dynamics, economic theories, financial principles
- [**Sociology**](docs/Sociology.md) — Social structures, collective behaviors, systemic patterns
- [**Physics**](docs/Physics.md) — Fundamental forces, thermodynamics, quantum phenomena
- [**Biology**](docs/Biology.md) — Evolutionary processes, ecological interactions, life sciences
- [**Computer Science**](docs/ComputerScience.md) — Algorithms, computing paradigms, information systems
- [**Management**](docs/Management.md) — Organizational behavior, leadership principles, productivity laws
- [**Marketing**](docs/Marketing.md) — Consumer psychology, persuasion effects, market responses
- [**Philosophy**](docs/Philosophy.md) — Epistemological rules, logical laws, ethical frameworks
- [**Mathematics**](docs/Mathematics.md) — Theorems, axioms, statistical and analytical principles

Additional domains—including medicine, environmental science, education, and emerging technologies—are under active development.

## 📖 Structure of Each Entry

Each law or effect in this repository follows a standardized format to ensure completeness and clarity:

### **Name** (Original Language + Translation)
- **Description**: Detailed explanation of the concept (minimum 200–300 words)
- **Origin**: Discoverer/inventor, year, historical context, and original publication
- **Wikipedia Links**: English and Chinese references for further reading
- **Applications**: 3–5 real-world scenarios across different industries or disciplines
- **Related Concepts**: Cross-references to related laws/effects within PhenomenaHub
- **Additional Notes**: Controversies, variations, experimental evidence, modern extensions

## 📝 Content Standards

All content in PhenomenaHub must:
- ✅ Be **factually accurate** and grounded in reliable sources
- ✅ Remain **objective**, avoiding personal or cultural bias
- ✅ Include **proper citations**, especially for academic or historical claims
- ✅ Provide **practical applications** with concrete examples
- ✅ Offer both **English and Chinese** versions with accurate translations
- ✅ Note **controversies or limitations** where applicable

## 🌐 Language Versions
